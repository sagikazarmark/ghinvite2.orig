# Reviewed email addresses outlive the creating browser

A reviewed batch belongs to its account, and another currently authorized
administrator may submit it. The creating browser's session-bound recovery plan
cannot provide that behavior. Keep that protection intact and add a distinct
encrypted review-address store, with a registered purpose binding each opaque
reference to its original batch, row, account and organization installation.

Review addresses have an immutable one-hour expiry. Only the web module that
checks current authority over every retained target may recover them, solely to
stage the exact selected email commands into delivery recovery. Its interface
validates and decrypts the entire selection before the first staging write;
escrow plaintext never returns through Restate, browser responses, audit, or
ordinary projections. The creating browser may still render the labels from its
separate session-bound input plan. A missing, expired or mismatched row refuses staging, preserves the
original operation, and gives fixed review-expiry guidance.

Delivery staging keeps its existing purpose, activation and retention rules.
Removed rows never enter delivery recovery; their review ciphertext can be
cleared after the authoritative removal receipt. Uncertain staging or submission
acknowledgements retain the original selected review records until their fixed
expiry, so retries cannot change references or extend retention. Bounded hourly
cleanup clears expired ciphertext and keeps non-reassignable reference tombstones.
Personal-link capabilities remain bound to the browser that allocated the exact
submission and are displayed at most once; another admin cannot recover them.

This explicitly amends ID-61 and P1 §11.4. It avoids both an original-browser-only
submission restriction and sharing the existing browser continuation scope.
